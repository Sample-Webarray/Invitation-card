What to Wear

Dress code

Traditional & Ethnic Wear

👔 Boys' Dress Code

  • Kurta Pajama
  • Colorful Shirt & Pant (avoid white or black shirt)

Choose any outfit that is comfortable and gives a formal, traditional look.

T-Shirts, Lower & Casual Wear are strictly not allowed.

👗 Girls' Dress Code

  • Saree
  • Traditional Suit
  • Garba Dress
  • Ghagra Set

Choose any traditional outfit that is comfortable and elegant.

Sleeveless, Backless, Body-Showing & Cut-Out Dresses are strictly not allowed.

Please ensure your outfit maintains dignity and follows the traditional theme.

Important Notice

All attendees are requested to strictly follow the dress code. Please wear comfortable, elegant, and appropriate traditional attire.
